🧭 Geographic Layers

🌍 Earth's curvatureShortest routes on a sphere are great-circle paths.
🌬️ WindUpper-air winds can change flight time and route planning.
🌦️ WeatherStorms, visibility and other atmospheric conditions can affect operations.
🏔️ TerrainMountains and elevation influence airport location and aircraft performance.
🏙️ Human geographyPopulation, trade and cities help determine where major hubs develop.
🗺️ AirspacePolitical boundaries, restricted areas and traffic-management regions shape routes.

💡 Why doesn't a flight simply take a straight line?

On a flat map, the shortest route can look curved or strangely positioned. Aircraft operate on a spherical Earth, so long-distance navigation often follows a great-circle route. Actual routes can then be adjusted for weather, winds, airspace, traffic and operational considerations.
